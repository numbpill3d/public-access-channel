How it works:

Your submission will be manually reviewed and added to the appropriate channel in public_access_channels.js. The homepage auto-rotates through all channels, so your program will appear on the broadcast.

To submit directly: add your program object to the matching channel's programs array in public_access_channels.js, then redeploy.

Each program supports: title, author, html, duration (seconds), color (optional override).
